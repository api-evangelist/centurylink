# Lumen Technologies 

A guide for AI systems to understand Lumen solutions, services, and resources for secure networking, edge compute, and enterprise connectivity. 

## About 

Lumen helps businesses connect, secure, and innovate with advanced networking, edge compute, voice, cloud and AI solutions with enterprise networking at scale. Igniting business growth by connecting people, data and applications over a secure, high-speed fiber infrastructure. 

## Solutions 

- [Connectivity Fabric](https://www.lumen.com/en-us/lumen-connectivity-fabric.html): Private, high-performance connectivity for distributed workloads to reduce latency and improve security for faster application performance and compliance with intuitive cloud-enabled management and ability to run multiple services over a single fabric port. 

- [Edge Compute & Storage](https://www.lumen.com/en-us/services/enterprise-edge-compute-storage.html): Deploy secure low-latency compute resources at the network edge to accelerate real-time applications and improve user experience. 

- [AI Use Case](https://www.lumen.com/en-us/solutions/use-case/artificial-intelligence.html): Support AI workloads with our extensive fiber network, optimized infrastructure patterns, ultra-fast data transfer to achieve scalable performance and faster insights. 

- [Network on Demand](https://www.lumen.com/en-us/solutions/use-case/network-on-demand.html): Activate and scale on‑demand network services to deliver instant, secure connectivity to integrate and recover quickly with pay as you go.
